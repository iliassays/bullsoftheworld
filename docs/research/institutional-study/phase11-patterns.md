# Phase 11 — Cross-Institution Patterns

**Date:** 2026-07-19. This phase synthesizes Phases 1–10; every pattern cites where its evidence
lives. Structure: (A) invariants — present in every documented survivor and absent or overridden
in every documented failure; (B) conditionals — practices that work *only* with named structural
preconditions; (C) myths the evidence killed; (D) the honest-uncertainty register.

## 11.A Invariants (the strongest claims this study can make)

1. **Downside first, examined by someone/something other than the idea's author.** Present in
   every survivor (Klarman's first question; PSH scenario framing; platform central risk; NBIM's
   Ministry-mandated TE budget; Sequoia's *post-reform* 4-to-1 rule). Absent or overridden in
   every failure: Amaranth (risk reports overridden — V, Senate), Archegos (no external risk
   process — V, court), Sequoia pre-reform (dissenters resigned — V), LTCM (model was the
   author). First flagged Phase 4; nothing in Phases 5–10 weakened it; multiple cases
   strengthened it. **This is the study's headline finding.**
2. **Pre-set caps separate conviction from catastrophe.** Every surviving concentrated book ran
   to a *written* ceiling (AmEx to the 40% Ground-Rule-7 limit; Chrysler to Magellan's 5%
   regulatory max; NBIM's 10% ownership cap; PSH's post-Valeant caps). Every sizing catastrophe
   was a cap breached or absent (Sequoia ~32% with the objection overridden; Archegos >10% of
   float; GME shorts >100% of float). Phase 5's finding, confirmed at trade level in Phase 10.
3. **Survival = catastrophe + reform, not catastrophe avoidance.** Every long-record institution
   has at least one disaster or drought (Phase 1 §obs 2), and **every fundamental survivor's
   current style is a post-failure style** (TCI post-2008, PSH post-Valeant, Greenlight
   post-2018, Bridgewater post-weak-decade, Citadel post-2008 — Phase 9). The reform is
   observable and structural (new rules, new capital terms), never just resolve.
4. **Denominator management.** The platform/quant tier *returns capital* as documented policy
   (Citadel $32bn; D.E. Shaw; Millennium; Medallion's ~$10bn cap; Bridgewater's deliberate
   shrink; Greenlight's re-closing — Phase 9). The RenTec internal/external divergence
   (Medallion +76% vs RIEF -19.9% in 2020) is the controlled experiment: same firm, different
   capacity discipline, opposite outcomes. No documented survivor let AUM float free of edge
   capacity.
5. **Pre-commitment devices, made expensive to break.** The mechanism recurs across otherwise
   unrelated survivors: Buffett's published acquisition criteria, Ackman's stone tablets, NBIM's
   votes published five days early, Yale's written policy portfolio, platform stop-outs that
   execute without appeal. The medium varies; the constant is that the rule exists *outside* the
   decision-maker's moment-of-temptation discretion. Druckenmiller 2000 ("I already knew…
   emotional basket case") is the counterfactual: world-class knowledge, no binding mechanism,
   -$3bn in six weeks.
6. **The failure signature is a conjunction, not a single sin:** concentration + leverage +
   liquidity mismatch + crowding, with governance unable to stop the trade (Phase 1 §obs 4).
   Every element alone is survivable (Berkshire concentrates; platforms lever; Yale is
   illiquid; momentum crowds). The documented deaths required the conjunction — which is why
   Phase 15's rulebook must be a *system* of caps, not a list of virtues.

## 11.B Conditionals (true only with the precondition — copying the practice without it is the documented failure mode)

| Practice | Works only with | Evidence pair (works / fails) |
|---|---|---|
| Thesis stops (no price stops) | Pre-modeled downside + no leverage + permanent capital | Buffett–AmEx / Miller 2008 (Phase 6) |
| Averaging down | All 7 conditions of the Phase 6 test | Berkshire–OXY / Ackman–Valeant |
| 40%+ concentration | Permanent or locked capital | Berkshire–Apple / Sequoia–Valeant |
| High gross leverage | Central risk + netting + termed financing | Millennium (SI) / LTCM, Archegos (V) |
| Multi-year shorts | Defined-risk structure or deep pockets + float math checked | MBIA via CDS / Melvin, VW shorts (Phase 10) |
| Holding through drawdowns | The position was sized to survive being wrong for years | AQR value (barely — fund -92% AUM) / Tiger 2022 |
| Illiquidity harvesting | Truly permanent liabilities + first-quartile access | Yale under Swensen / the average endowment copier (Ennis, Barber–Wang — Phase 9) |
| 13F cloning | Concentrated low-turnover single-CIO filers, top overweights only | Berkshire-mimic, Best Ideas / Griffin–Xu aggregate (Phase 8) |

The general law the table instantiates: **capital structure and governance are part of the
strategy.** Every "copy the great investor" failure in the record copied the position and not
the balance sheet behind it.

## 11.C Myths the evidence killed or wounded

1. **"The edge is secret information or secret formulas."** Phase 3: idea generation is the
   least differentiated stage; Phase 10: the win-column edge was careful reading of *public*
   documents (CAP white paper, bankruptcy math, pari passu clause, spin-off filings, buyback/
   float arithmetic). AQR's stated moat is implementation, not secrecy; DFA's verified edge
   (+2.2%/yr, Keim) was execution design. What is genuinely secret (Medallion) is genuinely
   unavailable — and honestly labeled U.
2. **"Great investors have great exits."** Phase 7 + 10: the exit problem is unsolved at every
   skill level (PetroChina "too soon," NVDA "big mistake," Burry-GME, Icahn halving Netflix).
   What survivors have is pre-registered *triggers*, and fidelity to them.
3. **"The average Magellan investor lost money."** Apocryphal — no primary source exists; the
   documented version (Lynch's own ~7%/yr vs the fund's 29%) is worse as a lesson and better as
   evidence: the behavior gap is the largest documented return leak in the study (Phase 9).
4. **"Platform risk rules are public knowledge."** The -5%/-7.5% folklore has zero primary
   documentation after a dedicated hunt (Phase 9) — the only primary-documented kill-switch
   grammar anywhere is in the RenTec basket-option contracts (Senate PSI, V).
5. **"Squeeze risk was unforeseeable before GME."** VW 2008 documented the full mechanics —
   derivative-hidden ownership, tiny float, >100% effective short interest, ~$30bn losses —
   twelve years earlier (Phase 10). Melvin's lesson was on the public record before Melvin
   existed. Institutional memory decays in about a decade; a rulebook must not.
6. **"Verified track records and spectacular track records are the same population."** Phase 1:
   the audited tier clusters at modest relative returns; the 15–20%+ CAGRs live almost entirely
   in the self-reported tier. Verifiability correlates inversely with claimed magnitude —
   which is itself a planning number for what Phase 12 should promise: little.

## 11.D The honest-uncertainty register (what this study still cannot say)

- Internal sizing math at every private fundamental shop (U across Phases 4–5).
- Platform pod-rule numbers (SI/WI — documented absence of primary sources).
- Exact P&L of most famous trades (Phase 10 §10.4) — Herbalife, BYD total, Burry-GME, Baupost.
- Medallion post-2024 anything (U).
- Whether 13F-cloning alpha survives post-2020 (no peer-reviewed re-test located — Phase 8).
- Whether insider-filing alpha survives modern filing speed (disputed — Phase 8).
- FIM per-trade cost tables (unverified from PDF — Phase 7). All queued for Phase 16.

## 11.E Implications handed to Phase 12 (system design constraints)

1. Every system must name its **structural asset** (retail's are: zero market impact, no
   redemption risk, no career risk, infinite patience — Phases 7/9) or assume it has none.
2. Every system must be a **conjunction-breaker**: at least one of
   {concentration, leverage, illiquidity, crowding} hard-capped at all times (11.A.6).
3. Every rule must live in a **pre-commitment medium** the operator can't quietly override
   (11.A.5) — written, versioned, with breaches logged (the repo itself is the stone tablet).
4. Sizing follows the **conditionals table**: no thesis-stops without the Buffett preconditions;
   at retail that means price/time stops by default (platform doctrine), because retail capital
   is permanent but retail *psychology* is not (the behavior gap, 11.C.3).
5. Benchmarks: NBIM's +0.24pp/yr relative and Contrafund's +2.9pp/yr are what verified
   excellence looks like at scale; DeMiguel's 1/N and a cap-weighted index are the honest nulls.
   Any Phase 12 paper book that can't beat the null after Patton–Weller costs gets parked —
   per the Atlas mandate, abstention is a result.
