## Sustainable weekly rhythm (after week 1)
- **Pre-open (Sun–Thu ~9:30):** "Today's watch" — repost Today's Standouts.
- **Post-close (~3:30 PM BST):** "Evening Wrap" — repost the daily wrap.
- **1–2× / week:** an educational explainer (use the in-app lessons).
- **Weekly (Thu/Fri):** week-in-review.
Always: end with the link + the no-advice line. Tie content to the market rhythm so people learn to check both FB and the app.

## Tip
Posts with an **image** get far more reach than plain links. Screenshot a stock page or the Evening Wrap for now; once the shareable stock-card PNG ships, every post gets an auto-generated branded image.
