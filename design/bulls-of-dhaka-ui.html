Choices

ThemeDark-first (OLED battery + premium trader feel); light theme as a toggle, not the default.
AccentBull Gold — wealth/premium, ties to the brand, and avoids colliding with the green/red up/down semantics.
Up / DownGreen up / red down — per-tenant configurable (other markets may flip).
TypeHind Siliguri (Bangla) + Inter (Latin). Tabular figures for all prices so digits align.
MotionSpring micro-interactions on like/sentiment; prices flash up/down on tick. Transforms & opacity only.
LoadingSkeleton placeholders, never spinners. Optimistic UI on post/like.
SkipHeavy glassmorphism / backdrop-blur everywhere — it tanks low-end Android GPUs.
